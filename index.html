FAQ

Fair questions.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. There's no lock-in contract and no notice period. You pay a month in advance, and if you decide to stop, we finish out the month you've already paid for and that's it. On the founding offer, your A$199 price is locked for 12 months as long as you stay with us.

How does billing work?

You pay a month in advance, in Australian dollars. If you stop, we finish out the month you've already paid for.

Who owns the website?

Your site goes live on a domain registered in your name, so your web address is yours. Your Facebook page stays yours too. We just need access to post on it for you.

What do you need from me?

Not much. Your business name, the work you do and the areas you cover, the phone number and email you want customers to use, any photos of your jobs, and access to your Facebook page. We sort out the rest.

What does the free mock-up cost?

Nothing. We make a mock-up of a new website for your business and a short sample ad. Have a look, and if it's not for you, no worries.

How does the advertising work?

Your monthly fee covers making your ad clips. Every business is different, so we'll talk it through and work out an ad budget that suits you. Money spent running ads is paid to the platform they run on, such as Facebook, Google or elsewhere, and is separate from the monthly fee.

What happens after the first 12 months?

Founding customers move to the normal price of A$299 a month after 12 months. There's still no lock-in.
