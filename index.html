FAQ

Fair questions.

What's the difference between the two options?

The Starter site is for brand new businesses that want a good website to get going. You pay A$449 upfront plus A$15 a month for hosting, or A$59 a month for 12 months with hosting included. Either way you get SSL security, backups and uptime monitoring, a Google review link and printable QR code card, and free email forwarding to your existing inbox. The Growth plan is for businesses that want it all done for them: your website, one social platform, your ads, your Google Business Profile, a real business mailbox and a monthly report, looked after every month for A$299 a month (A$199 for our first 5 clients).

Is there a lock-in contract?

The Growth plan has a 6-month minimum, which covers the cost of building your website. After that it's month to month, and if you decide to stop, we finish out the month you've already paid for and that's it. A Starter site paid upfront has no minimum, just A$15 a month hosting to keep it online. If you're paying a Starter site off monthly, it's 12 payments, and if you want to leave early you just pay out what's left.

How does billing work?

Monthly fees are paid a month in advance, in Australian dollars. The Starter site is either a single A$449 payment plus A$15 a month hosting, or A$59 a month for 12 months with hosting included. One-off add-ons are paid when the work is done. Your domain has a small yearly fee paid directly by you.

Who owns the website?

Your domain is always yours, because it's registered in your name. Your social pages stay yours too. We just need access to post for you. A Starter site paid upfront is yours from the start. If you pay it off monthly, it's yours after your 12th payment. On the Growth plan, your website build is covered by the 6-month minimum, and once you've finished it, the site is yours to keep. If you leave the plan after that, we can keep it online for A$15 a month hosting.

Do I get a business email?

On a Starter site, you get free email forwarding. Addresses like info@ and bookings@ on your own domain are forwarded to the inbox you already use, so you can receive email at them, but replies come from your existing address. On the Growth plan, you get a real business mailbox you can send from, like you@yourbusiness.com.au, plus up to 5 more for your team, so up to 6 in total. Each one is a separate mailbox with its own login, all on your domain, like info@, bookings@ or a staff member's name. We set them up and look after them, and they work on your phone and computer. Either way, it's on your own domain, so it's yours. If you'd like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 instead, we can upgrade you for about A$16 per mailbox a month, billed by us.

What's the review QR code card?

It's a printable PDF with your Google review link and a QR code on it. Print it yourself and pop it on your counter, in your van or on the back of an invoice. Customers scan it with their phone and go straight to leaving you a review. It's included with both options.

What's in the monthly report?

One page, in plain English: how many people visited your website, how far your social posts reached, and how many enquiries came through. There's nothing to log in to. It just lands in your inbox.

Which social platforms do you look after?

On the Growth plan, you pick one: Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, whichever your customers use most. We post up to 16 times a month (4 a week) and run your ads there. Want more than one? Each extra platform is A$94 a month on the Growth plan, or A$142 a month on its own.

What are the add-ons?

Extras you can add to either option, or get on their own: an extra social platform (A$94 a month on the Growth plan, A$142 a month on its own), short-form videos (4 Reels or TikToks for A$142 a month), online booking setup (A$94 one-off), extra website pages (A$47 each), a logo refresh (A$142 one-off) and Google Business Profile setup (A$142 one-off, for Starter sites, as it's already included in Growth).

Can my customers book online?

Yes, if you want them to. Online booking setup is a one-off add-on for A$94, using a free booking app, so customers can pick a time that suits them.

What if I need changes to my website later?

On the Growth plan, small changes are included on a fair use basis, like updating your prices, hours, photos or a bit of text. Bigger changes, like a new section or a redesign, are booked in as a refresh and priced on what the job needs. On a Starter site, extra pages are A$47 each, and anything else we'll quote as a small one-off job before we start.

What do you need from me?

Not much. Your business name, the work you do and the areas you cover, the phone number and email you want customers to use, any photos of your jobs, and access to your social page and Google Business Profile if you're on the Growth plan. We sort out the rest.

What does the free mock-up cost?

Nothing. We make a mock-up of a new website for your business and a short sample ad. Have a look, and if it's not for you, no worries.

How does the advertising work?

On the Growth plan, your monthly fee covers setting up and managing your ads on your chosen platform. Every business is different, so we'll talk it through and work out an ad budget that suits you. Money spent running ads is paid to the platform they run on, such as Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, and is separate from the monthly fee.

What happens after the first 12 months?

On the Growth plan, founding clients move to the normal price of A$299 a month after 12 months, still month to month. If you paid off a Starter site monthly, it's all yours after 12 payments and you just pay A$15 a month hosting from then on.
