Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what happens to your details when you use this website or get in touch with Hard to Miss Marketing.

This website doesn't track you

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. We don't use Google Analytics or a Facebook pixel. The fonts and images are stored on this site, so loading a page doesn't send your details to anyone else.

Like most websites, our web host may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. We don't use those logs to work out who you are.

The contact form

The form on our home page doesn't send anything to this website and nothing is saved here. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with your details filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

What we collect when you contact us

If you call, text, email or message us on Facebook, we receive whatever you choose to share, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address and details about your business.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to make your free mock-up if you ask for one, and, if you become a customer, to run your marketing and send you invoices. We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who else sees it

We only share your details when we need to do the work, for example with the services we use to host your website or post to your Facebook page, or if the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable care to keep your details secure and only keep them for as long as we need them.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your details

You can ask what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Just get in touch.
