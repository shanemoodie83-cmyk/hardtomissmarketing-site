4Can I use your car for my driving test?+

Yes. With the test package I pick you up, give you a warm-up lesson on the local test routes, then you sit the test in the car you've been learning in. You book the test itself with the licensing office, and I'll help you find a time that works.

It depends on how much driving you do at home. Someone who practises with a parent most weeks might only need a handful of lessons to get test-ready. A complete beginner will need more. After your first lesson I'll give you an honest number, not an upsell.
